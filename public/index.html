    Heart-rate profile & method

    How these are calculated

    VO2 max compares the oxygen cost of each run's pace (Daniels' equation, with a hill correction) with the share of your heart-rate reserve it took. The chart shows the upper quartile of the last six weeks, since heat, hills and fatigue only drag single runs down. VDOT is the same scale computed from your best performance alone.

    Training load uses heart rate (Banister TRIMP) when available and pace relative to threshold otherwise; one hour at threshold is about 100 points. Fitness and fatigue are 42- and 7-day weighted averages of daily load.

    Readiness weighs form (40%), the load ramp (25%), time since your last hard run (20%) and your weekly check-in (15%). These are models for planning, not medical measurements.
